## 📊 Understanding the Data

VisTouch provides a complete look at how robots interact with surfaces. The dataset includes high-speed video, pressure readings from sensors, and sound files captured during contact. Researchers use this information to teach robots how to grasp objects without dropping them. The interface allows you to zoom into individual frames of video to see how the surface changes under pressure. This level of detail helps users see the difference between soft and hard textures during a sliding movement.
